PORTAL DESCRIPTION / COLLEGE INFORMATION

StudentHub Portal is a comprehensive student management platform designed to streamline academic scheduling, course access, event updates, placement tracking, and scholarship information for students and faculty.


VERSION: 1.0.0

CONTACT: SUPPORT@STUDENTHUB.EDU


FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Click on the TIME TABLE card from the dashboard to view daily class times, lunch break schedules, and practical lab allocations.

Visit the SCHOLARSHIP DETAILS section to review criteria, eligibility norms, and annual grant amounts. Contact the administration desk to submit your documents.

Go to PLACEMENT RECORDS to view company hiring statistics, highest and average packages for TCS, Infosys, Amazon, and others.

Navigate to SETTING from the home page and click the Theme switch button.
